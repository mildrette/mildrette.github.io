Who I Am

About Me

I am a self-taught developer based in Bamenda, Cameroon. I have built the entire web presence of SMSWithoutBorders and Afkanerd — 6+ live websites, a documentation platform maintained solo for 3+ years, a telemetry dashboard, and Android app contributions.

I started with HTML and CSS, taught myself JavaScript and React, and am now learning Kotlin and Jetpack Compose as a Junior Android Developer. Every skill I have was built by doing real work for real teams.

I am the kind of developer who takes ownership — when I took over the SMSWithoutBorders documentation in 2022, I have updated every single part of it. When I joined the Android team, I shipped real instrumentation tests to a 7-year-old production codebase.

I dream of contributing to open source competitions, traveling internationally, and building a remote career that lets me grow as far as my potential can take me.
