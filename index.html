About me

I like taking something from an idea or design and turning it into software people can use.

I am a software developer with practical experience working on Android applications, frontend interfaces, testing, localization and open-source projects.

My Android experience includes Kotlin and Jetpack Compose, including implementing interfaces from Figma designs, writing instrumentation and unit tests, and contributing translations to production applications.

On the web, I work with HTML, CSS, JavaScript, React.js, Next.js, Bootstrap and Material UI.

I have contributed to projects across the Afkanerd and SMSWithoutBorders ecosystems, including RelaySMS, DekaSMS, LibSMSMMS, websites and documentation projects.

Currently, I am strengthening my Python skills and moving toward backend development, APIs and databases, while continuing to improve my Kotlin and Jetpack Compose skills.
